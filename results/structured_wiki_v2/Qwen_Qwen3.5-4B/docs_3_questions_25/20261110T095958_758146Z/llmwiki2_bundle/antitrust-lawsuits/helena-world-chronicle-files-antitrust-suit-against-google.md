Helena World Chronicle, an Arkansas publisher owning two weekly newspapers, filed a class action lawsuit in the U.S. District Court in D.C. against Google and Alphabet, alleging anticompetitive behavior under the Sherman Act. The suit claims Google and Alphabet siphon off publishers' content, readers, and ad revenue through anticompetitive means, specifically citing AI technologies such as Search Generative Experience (SGE) and the Bard AI chatbot.

The complaint argues that Google is starving the free press by sharing publishers' content on its platforms, causing them to lose billions of dollars. It alleges that Google's Knowledge Graph, launched in May 2012 and containing 500 billion facts by 2020, misappropriated information from publishers' websites and open-source databases. Additionally, the suit cites a 2023 News Media Alliance report and a Washington Post article stating that Google's Bard AI was trained on news, magazine, and digital publications, with news sites being the third-largest category of AI training data.

The case alleges changing AdSense rates and improper spoliation of evidence via the destruction of chat messages, referencing the recent Epic Games antitrust win. The lawsuit requests an injunction requiring Google to obtain publisher consent to use website data for training AI products and allows publishers opting out of SGE to appear in search results. This U.S. lawsuit coincides with the Justice Department's suit against Google for monopolizing digital ad technologies.

Plaintiff Helena World Chronicle, LLC invokes the Sherman Act and Clayton Act, stating that Google's scheme harms competition, consumers, labor, and a democratic free press. A Google spokesperson called the lawsuit meritless, claiming Google links people to publisher websites more than 24 billion times monthly at no cost. Google reached a Canadian agreement paying $73.5 million (100 million CAD) annually to news organizations based on headcount, while negotiations with Meta remain unresolved after Meta blocked Canadian news in August.
