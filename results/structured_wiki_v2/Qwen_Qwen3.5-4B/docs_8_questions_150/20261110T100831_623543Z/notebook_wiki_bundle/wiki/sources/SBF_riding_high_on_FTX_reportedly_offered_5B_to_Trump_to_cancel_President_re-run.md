# SBF, riding high on FTX, reportedly offered $5B to Trump to cancel President re-run

## Core Content

This document details the upcoming trial of Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) regarding the collapse of FTX and Alameda Research, highlighting Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite' which chronicles SBF's rise and alleged attempts to buy off Donald Trump with a $5 billion offer. The text contrasts SBF's positive media coverage and perceived idealism with public outrage over the financial fraud, noting his political maneuvering and the subsequent crash of cryptocurrency valuations on the platform.

## Key Points

- Sam Bankman-Fried is facing trial on seven charges of fraud and conspiracy related to the collapse of FTX.
- Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite' details SBF's rise, his relationship with Lewis, and an alleged $5 billion offer to Donald Trump to cancel his presidential run.
- SBF engaged in significant political maneuvering, meeting with Mitch McConnell and seeking to fund candidates on both sides of the aisle.
- The document highlights public criticism of SBF and the media's coverage, contrasting it with the financial reality of the FTX collapse.
- SBF's company, FTX, collapsed after cryptocurrency valuations fell, leading to accusations of fraud and conspiracy.

## Facts and Data

- SBF pleaded not-guilty to seven charges of fraud and conspiracy.
- The trial is set to take place in Manhattan Federal Court.
- Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite' is set to debut on the first day of the trial.
- SBF raised approximately $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from backers.
- Backers included Sequoia, Temasek, and Tiger Global.
- SBF was the richest person in the world under 30 at the time.
- SBF met with Mitch McConnell to discuss funding political candidates.
- Tom Brady was paid $55 million for a marketing deal with FTX.
- Steph Curry was paid $35 million for a marketing deal with FTX.
- Anna Wintour contacted SBF to sponsor the Met Costume Ball.
- FTX trade volume climbed to $15 billion per day.
- SBF was denied bail.
- SBF met with Michael Lewis more than 100 times over two years.
- Ayelet Noff is the CEO and founder of Sliced Brand.
- Alameda Research is the sister company to FTX.
- The trial involves allegations of orchestrating one of the biggest financial frauds in American history.
- Michael Lewis wrote 'Liar's Poker', 'Moneyball', and 'The Big Short'.
