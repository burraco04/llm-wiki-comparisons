Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) is currently on trial at Manhattan Federal Court for orchestrating the collapse of FTX, a crypto exchange he founded. He pleaded not-guilty to seven charges of fraud and conspiracy. During this period, SBF reportedly offered Donald Trump $5 billion to cancel his presidential re-run; however, no wire transfer was confirmed. This political fundraising plan was part of a broader strategy where SBF met with Mitch McConnell to discuss funding Republican candidates to offset Trump's influence. SBF had previously raised approximately $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from backers including Sequoia, Temasek, and Tiger Global. Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite" is set to debut on the first day of the trial, detailing their extensive relationship which included over 100 meetings. While SBF sought to leverage political capital, the FTX cryptocurrency valuations eventually fell sharply after being based on speculation rather than banked value, leading to SBF's denial of bail and significant criticism from investors and industry figures like Ayelet Noff.
