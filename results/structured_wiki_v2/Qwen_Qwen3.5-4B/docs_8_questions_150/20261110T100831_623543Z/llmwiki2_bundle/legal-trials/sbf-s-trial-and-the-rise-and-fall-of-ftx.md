Sam Bankman-Fried co-founded Alameda Research in 2017 and FTX in 2019 as a complement to it. Within two years of FTX's founding, over 80 investors provided approximately $2 billion in capital. In January 2022, FTX raised $400 million in a Series C round, boosting its valuation to $32 billion. That same year, FTX bought the naming rights for the Miami Heat's home arena and partnered with celebrities including Tom Brady, Gisele Bündchen, Steph Curry, Shaquille O'Neal, and Naomi Osaka.

CoinDesk published Alameda's balance sheet showing $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022. Alameda's largest assets were $3.66 billion of 'unlocked FTT' and $2.16 billion of 'FTT collateral'. Alameda owned $5.82 billion in FTT tokens, which was 193% higher than the total FTT market cap of about $3 billion. Binance pulled out $2.1 billion equivalent of cash in BUSD and FTT, triggering a bank run on FTX.

FTX and Alameda filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the U.S. in mid-November 2022. Sam Bankman-Fried resigned as CEO and John J. Ray III was appointed the new CEO. SBF maintained his innocence, stating he was shocked by what happened and regretted filing for bankruptcy.

SBF was arrested in the Bahamas in December 2022, extradited to the U.S., and released on $250 million bail; his house arrest in Palo Alto was revoked in August following a diary leak accusation. During testimony, John J. Ray III stated that House Financial Services had virtually no internal controls, no separateness, and did not trust a single piece of paper. U.S. Attorney Damian Williams called the crimes the biggest financial fraud in American history.

Gary Wang and Caroline Ellison pleaded guilty in December 2022 to federal charges and face civil penalties from the SEC and CFTC, planning to cooperate as witnesses. SBF pleaded not guilty in January to wire fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering, and conspiracy to misuse customer funds, facing up to 115 years in jail if convicted on all charges. The FTX collapse was the first of many, with BlockFi filing for Chapter 11 in November 2022 and Genesis Global Trading filing in January.
