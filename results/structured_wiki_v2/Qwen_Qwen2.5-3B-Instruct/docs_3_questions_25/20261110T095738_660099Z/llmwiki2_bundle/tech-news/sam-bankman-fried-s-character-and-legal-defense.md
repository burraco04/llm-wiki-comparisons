The prosecution presents Sam Bankman-Fried as a liar and fraud, accusing him of stealing customer funds and concealing transactions. In contrast, his lawyer argues that Alameda was a successful hedge fund and FTX was a successful company with reasonable business practices. Bankman-Fried emphasizes that businesses can fail but he did not steal money. The defense includes two key witnesses: Marc-Antoine Julliard, who attempted to withdraw funds but was unsuccessful, and Adam Yedidia, who testified that he wrote code contributing to a crime and received legal immunity for his testimony.
