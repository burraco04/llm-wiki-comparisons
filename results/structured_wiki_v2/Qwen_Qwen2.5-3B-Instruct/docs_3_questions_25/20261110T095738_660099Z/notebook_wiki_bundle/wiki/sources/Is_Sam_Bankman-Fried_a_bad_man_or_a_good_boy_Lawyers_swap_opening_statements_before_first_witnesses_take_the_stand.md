# Is Sam Bankman-Fried a bad ‘man’ or a good ‘boy’? Lawyers swap opening statements before first witnesses take the stand

## Core Content

This document discusses the ongoing trial of Sam Bankman-Fried, the former CEO of FTX, focusing on contrasting narratives presented by the prosecution and defense. The prosecution portrays Bankman-Fried as a liar and fraud, while the defense argues he acted in good faith.

## Key Points

- Sam Bankman-Fried is the subject of a trial over alleged crimes related to FTX and Alameda Research
- The prosecution claims Bankman-Fried stole customer funds and lied to investors
- The defense argues Bankman-Fried acted in good faith and did not defraud anyone

## Facts and Data

- Bankman-Fried was the CEO of FTX and oversaw a crypto exchange that allegedly stole customer funds
- The trial features pointed allegations and witnesses from both sides
- Marc-Antoine Julliard, a FTX investor, testified about losing almost $100,000
- Adam Yedidia, a former colleague of Bankman-Fried, testified about potentially writing code that contributed to a crime
- The trial involves multiple witnesses, including a former partner at Sequoia Capital and a key Bankman-Fried lieutenant
- The case highlights the complex nature of the cryptocurrency industry and the challenges faced by startups in this field
