# Notes

* [Google's AI Practices and Antitrust Suit Against News Publishers](google-s-ai-practices-and-antitrust-suit-against-news.md) - A class action lawsuit filed by news publishers accuses Google and Alphabet of anticompetitive practices, particularly regarding AI technologies used in Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE) and Bard AI chatbot. The lawsuit alleges misuse of publishers' content and ad revenue, and seeks an injunction to prevent improper use of publisher data for AI training.
* [Apple's Defense of Google Search Deal in Court](apple-s-defense-of-google-search-deal-in-court.md) - Apple's defense in the US v. Google antitrust trial includes arguments about user experience, privacy, and the necessity of Google's services in Apple's ecosystem.
