In 2018, Google made a proposal to preinstall Google services on every iOS device, which would have allowed Google to collect more data and potentially increase revenue. This move was seen as a way to strengthen Google's position in the search market. However, Apple rejected the idea, citing concerns about user privacy and control over their devices.
