FTX secured branding deals and partnerships with major brands and celebrities, including the Miami Heat, MLB umpires, Tom Brady, Gisele Bündchen, and Steph Curry, among others. By June 30, 2022, FTX's balance sheet showed $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities, with $5.82 billion in FTT tokens, indicating an inflated asset value. In early November 2022, FTX faced a liquidity crisis due to a discrepancy between its reported assets and the actual market value of FTT tokens, leading to a bank run and subsequent bankruptcy filing. Sam Bankman-Fried was arrested in December 2022 in the Bahamas, where FTX was based. He was extradited to the U.S. to face criminal charges. Bankman-Fried was released on a $250 million bail bond and remained under house arrest at his parents' home in Palo Alto.
