FTX, founded by Sam Bankman-Fried and Alameda Research in 2019, attracted significant investment and grew rapidly. By 2022, FTX had a valuation of $32 billion and raised over $2 billion in capital. However, FTX faced a liquidity crisis due to discrepancies in its reported assets and token values, leading to a bank run and bankruptcy. Key events include Sam Bankman-Fried's arrest in December 2022, his bail bond of $250 million, and the subsequent legal proceedings involving Gary Wang and Caroline Ellison.

# Sam Bankman-Fried's Financial and Political Interactions

Michael Lewis met Sam Bankman-Fried in early 2021 through a friend who wanted to invest in FTX. Despite no financial commitment, Lewis was impressed by SBF's potential and decided to write a book about him. Sam Bankman-Fried raised $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from various investors like Sequoia, Temasek, and Tiger Global. This investment coincided with SBF's rapid rise to becoming the richest person under 30 with $22.5 billion. Michael Lewis recounts SBF's efforts to influence political power through discussions with Mitch McConnell and potential offers to Trump and Democratic candidates. SBF considered offering $5 billion to Trump to prevent him from running for president again. The note also covers the collapse of FTX and the subsequent hard crash, during which SBF was denied bail and faced criticism from various parties. Industry experts criticized SBF's management skills and the impact of his actions on the crypto community, including potential mismanagement and manipulation.
