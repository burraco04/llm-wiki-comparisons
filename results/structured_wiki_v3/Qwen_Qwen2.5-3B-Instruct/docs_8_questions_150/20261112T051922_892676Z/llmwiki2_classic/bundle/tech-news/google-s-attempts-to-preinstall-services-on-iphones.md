In 2018, Google made efforts to persuade Apple to preinstall Google services on every iOS device. This was aimed at increasing revenue for Apple by integrating Google services directly into the operating system and boosting Google's search traffic. Despite these attempts, Apple declined to implement this plan, leading to a significant setback for Google's strategy.
