<p>Eddy Cue, Apple’s senior vice president of Internet services and commerce, testified that Apple believed in both protecting user privacy and supporting Google. He emphasized that Apple never seriously considered switching to another search engine or developing its own, stating that Google was the best choice at the time and remains so. During the renegotiation of the Information Services Agreement (ISA), Apple sought a higher revenue share, but ultimately agreed to a compromise. Apple maintains that the deal was beneficial for both parties and that there was no viable alternative to Google at the time.</p>

<li>Apple argues against offering users a choice of search engines during setup due to a bad user experience.</li>

<li>Apple emphasizes the importance of user privacy and cites Google's privacy policies as reasons for the deal.</li>
<li>Apple mentions that the integration of Google services into Safari was a user interface innovation.</li>
