A class action lawsuit has been filed in D.C. District Court accusing Google and Alphabet of anticompetitive behavior, primarily due to the use of AI technologies such as Search Generative Experience (SGE) and Bard AI chatbot. The lawsuit highlights the potential loss of traffic for publishers, estimating a reduction of 20-40% of their website traffic due to these AI technologies. It also mentions concerns about AdSense rates and improper spoliation of evidence, including the destruction of chat messages. Additionally, the lawsuit references the 2020 Justice Department's civil antitrust suit over search and search advertising, distinguishing it from the more recent suit against digital ad technologies. Google denies the lawsuit's merit, arguing that people can access information and news content through various means, including publishers' websites, dedicated apps, social media platforms, print papers, and more.
