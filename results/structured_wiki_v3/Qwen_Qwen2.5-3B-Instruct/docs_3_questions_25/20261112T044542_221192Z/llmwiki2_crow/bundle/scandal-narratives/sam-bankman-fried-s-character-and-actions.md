# Opening Statements and Narratives

The prosecution portrays Sam Bankman-Fried as a liar and fraud, while his defense argues he acted in good faith. Key points include the alleged theft of customer funds, mismanagement of Alameda Research, and the portrayal of Bankman-Fried as a 'man' versus a 'boy'.

## Bankman-Fried's Defense

Bankman-Fried argues that Alameda was a successful hedge fund and FTX was a successful company with reasonable business practices.

## Julliard's Testimony

Marc-Antoine Julliard, a FTX investor, testified that he could not withdraw his funds or crypto from FTX on November 8, 2021.

## Yedidia's Testimony

Adam Yedidia, a former colleague of Bankman-Fried, testified that he wrote code contributing to a crime and received legal immunity for his testimony.
