News publishers have filed a class action lawsuit against Google and Alphabet, alleging anticompetitive behavior involving AI technologies such as Google's Bard AI and Search Generative Experience (SGE). The lawsuit emphasizes the impact of these technologies on publishers' bottom lines, including improper spoliation of evidence and concerns about AdSense rates. It seeks an injunction requiring Google to obtain publisher consent for AI training and to allow publishers opting out of SGE to maintain visibility in Google search results. Additionally, the lawsuit references a settlement agreement between Google and the Canadian government, which includes annual payments to news organizations based on their headcount.
