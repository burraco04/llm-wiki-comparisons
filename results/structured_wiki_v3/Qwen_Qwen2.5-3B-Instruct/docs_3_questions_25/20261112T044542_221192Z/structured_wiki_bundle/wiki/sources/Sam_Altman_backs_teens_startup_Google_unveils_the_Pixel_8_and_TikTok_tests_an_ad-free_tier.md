# Sam Altman backs teens’ startup, Google unveils the Pixel 8 and TikTok tests an ad-free tier

## Core Content

This week's tech news includes Sam Altman backing a teen's AI startup, Google unveiling the Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro, Flexport's turmoil, the FTX fallout, Gmail's stricter spam rules, TikTok's ad-free subscription plan, LinkedIn's AI tools, Elon Musk's X metrics, and the closure of IronNet.

## Key Points

- Sam Altman supports a teen-founded AI startup
- Google launches Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro
- Flexport CEO Dave Clark criticizes the board and founder
- FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried faces trial
- TikTok introduces an ad-free subscription service
- LinkedIn integrates AI into its recruitment and marketing tools
- Elon Musk's X platform sees conflicting user activity numbers
- IronNet, a cybersecurity startup, closes and lays off staff

## Facts and Data

- Induced AI, backed by Sam Altman, assists businesses in automating workflows using plain English inputs.
- The Pixel 8 Pro offers improved cameras, brighter screens, and enhanced AI features compared to the Pixel 7 Pro.
- Flexport's CEO, Dave Clark, was ousted a year after taking the CEO role.
- FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried faces charges of orchestrating the collapse of cryptocurrency exchange FTX.
- TikTok plans to test an ad-free subscription tier for some users, priced at $4.99.
- LinkedIn's AI features include an AI-powered talent sourcing platform, LinkedIn Learning coach, and marketing campaign tool.
- Elon Musk claims X users generate 100 million to 200 million posts daily, but X CEO Linda Yaccarino reports 500 million posts per day.
- IronNet, founded by a former NSA director, collapsed and laid off its staff following its failure to gain traction.
