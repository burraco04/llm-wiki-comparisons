# Sam Altman backs teens’ startup, Google unveils the Pixel 8 and TikTok tests an ad-free tier

## Core Content

This TechCrunch Week in Review highlights major tech developments including Sam Altman's support for a teen-founded AI startup, Google's hardware event showcasing the Pixel 8 series, and TikTok's experimental ad-free subscription. The digest also covers significant corporate turmoil at Flexport, emerging details from the Sam Bankman-Fried trial, and various industry shifts such as LinkedIn's AI integration and Gmail's new anti-spam measures. Additionally, it notes conflicting reports on X's daily post volume and the shutdown of former NSA director Keith Alexander's cybersecurity firm, IronNet.

## Key Points

- Sam Altman is backing Induced AI, a startup founded by teenagers that automates business workflows using plain English inputs.
- Google unveiled the Pixel 8, Pixel 8 Pro, and Pixel Fold at its annual hardware event, with the Pro receiving praise for its camera and screen improvements.
- TikTok is testing an ad-free subscription tier for $4.99 in a single non-U.S. English-speaking market.
- Flexport CEO Dave Clark criticized the company's founder and board following reports about his ousting and the firm's $8 billion valuation.
- New details from Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite' suggest Sam Bankman-Fried attempted to buy off Donald Trump during the FTX trial.

## Facts and Data

- Induced AI was founded this year.
- Induced AI converts back-office tasks from plain English to pseudo-code in real time.
- The Pixel 8 Pro is similar to the Pixel 7 Pro but features improved cameras and a brighter screen.
- Google's hardware event also announced updates to Android 14, Pixel Buds, and the Pixel Watch 2.
- Flexport is a freight forwarding and customs brokerage startup valued at $8 billion.
- Dave Clark was ousted as CEO of Flexport just one year into his job.
- Clark made comments about Flexport on social media site X.
- The SBF trial is taking place in the Manhattan Federal Court.
- Michael Lewis wrote the book 'Going Infinite' which debuted on the first day of the SBF trial.
- Gmail announced changes requiring bulk senders to authenticate emails starting next year.
- TikTok's ad-free tier costs $4.99 per month.
- TikTok is piloting its ad-free plan in a single English-speaking market outside the U.S.
- LinkedIn unveiled new AI features for its Recruiter, Learning, and marketing products.
- Elon Musk previously stated X users generate 100 to 200 million posts daily.
- X CEO Linda Yaccarino claimed X sees 500 million posts per day.
- IronNet was founded in 2014 by retired four-star general Keith Alexander.
- IronNet raised more than $400 million in funding.
- IronNet went public in August 2021.
- IronNet is based in Virginia.
- TechCrunch's Week in Review column is published every Saturday.
- The SBF trial involves the prosecution of fraud charges against Sam Bankman-Fried.
- The defense in the SBF trial argues Bankman-Fried acted in good faith.
- Fearless Fund is a firm barred from awarding grants to Black women founders.
- Breathe Battery Technologies developed software to improve lithium-ion battery charging and longevity.
