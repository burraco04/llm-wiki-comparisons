# Is Google Search better than the rest? And is that fair?

## Core Content

The document explores the debate over Google's dominance in search, contrasting its data collection and personalization capabilities with alternatives like DuckDuckGo and Bing. It examines legal arguments regarding Google's default status, allegations of query manipulation for ad revenue, and the technical processes behind search ranking, including the massive scale of data indexed and the use of machine learning.

## Key Points

- Google's extensive data collection on searches and interactions is a primary advantage for personalization but a point of contention for privacy advocates.
- Legal challenges to Google's default status face hurdles because users often actively choose Google over other providers despite initial defaults.
- Allegations exist that Google manipulates search queries to maximize ad revenue, though Google denies altering organic results.
- The volume of indexed data has grown exponentially from 9 ZB in 2013 to over 120 ZB in 2023, changing the nature of search results.
- Google utilizes machine learning and semantic matching to refine queries, while critics argue this widens the content pool to favor monetization.

## Facts and Data

- Google indexed 9 ZB of data in 2013.
- Google indexed over 120 ZB of data in 2023.
- Google's central index is larger than 100 million gigabytes.
- Google ran almost 900,000 quality tests in 2022.
- Google Search bar is used for returning to familiar sites as much as finding new information.
- Bing Chat offered comparisons for 'kids BMX bike green' but recommended options over $300.
- 'Google' was the number one search query on Bing in 2021.
- Megan Gray is a former vice-president at DuckDuckGo.
- Wired posted an opinion article alleging Google manipulates search queries.
- Loading Google rejected the Wired article as misleading and inaccurate.
- Google denies inferring personal information like race or religion to shape results.
- Ad keyword matching is a separate process from organic result matching according to Google.
- Users can turn Google's personalization features off in 'Web & App Activity' settings.
- Kent Walker is the president of global affairs at Google.
- Yahoo! was made the default on Firefox by Mozilla during a trial.
- Most users changed the Firefox default back to Google after the Yahoo! trial.
- Google Search results include blue links that are not sponsored.
- Google Search looks beyond specific words to understand query meaning.
- DuckDuckGo ignored the 'green' part of the 'kids BMX bike green' search query.
- The tech industry is described as intensely competitive and dynamic.
