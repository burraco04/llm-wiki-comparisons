# SBF’s trial starts soon, but how did he — and FTX — get here?

## Core Content

Sam Bankman-Fried's criminal trial for seven counts of fraud and conspiracy against FTX has begun, following the exchange's collapse in November 2022 due to severe liquidity issues and mismanagement of customer funds. The once-valued $32 billion crypto empire filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, leading to the arrest of Bankman-Fried and the guilty pleas of former executives Gary Wang and Caroline Ellison, while the trial proceeds with high-profile legal representation.

## Key Points

- Sam Bankman-Fried is currently on trial for seven counts of fraud and conspiracy related to FTX.
- FTX collapsed in November 2022 after exposing a balance sheet showing impossible FTT token holdings.
- Bankman-Fried pleaded not guilty to all charges and faces up to 115 years in prison if convicted.
- Former executives Gary Wang and Caroline Ellison pleaded guilty and will serve as key witnesses.
- The trial is represented by Mark Cohen of Cohen & Gresser, who previously defended Ghislaine Maxwell.

## Facts and Data

- The trial started on a Tuesday.
- Bankman-Fried is 31 years old.
- FTX was co-founded in 2019.
- Alameda Research was co-founded in 2017.
- FTX's peak valuation was $32 billion.
- Over 80 investors provided $2 billion in capital to FTX.
- FTX raised $400 million in a Series C round in January 2022.
- FTX bought the naming rights for the Miami Heat's arena in 2021.
- Tom Brady and Gisele Bündchen were FTX brand partners.
- Steph Curry, Shaquille O'Neal, and Naomi Osaka were FTX partners.
- CoinDesk published Alameda's balance sheet in early November 2022.
- Alameda held $14.6 billion in assets and $8 billion in liabilities as of June 30, 2022.
- Alameda owned $5.82 billion in FTT tokens, exceeding the total market cap.
- Binance pulled out $2.1 billion in cash in BUSD and FTT.
- FTX and Alameda filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in mid-November 2022.
- John J. Ray III was appointed FTX's new CEO.
- Bankman-Fried was arrested in the Bahamas in December 2022.
- Bankman-Fried was released on a $250 million bail bond.
- Bankman-Fried was accused of intimidating Caroline Ellison in August.
- Gary Wang and Caroline Ellison pleaded guilty in December 2022.
- BlockFi filed for Chapter 11 in November 2022.
- Genesis Global Trading filed for Chapter 11 in January.
- Mark Cohen represented Bankman-Fried in the trial.
- Mark Cohen previously represented Ghislaine Maxwell.
