Sam Altman has backed Induced AI, a startup founded this year by two teenagers that automates business workflows by converting plain English instructions to pseudo-code in real time. Google unveiled the Pixel 8, Pixel 8 Pro, Pixel Fold, Android 14, Pixel Buds, Google Assistant, Bard, and Pixel Watch 2 at its annual hardware event. A review of the Pixel 8 Pro highlights improved cameras, a brighter screen, and enhanced AI features, though it notes an underutilized temperature sensor. LinkedIn launched new AI tools for job hunting, marketing, and sales, including an updated Recruiter platform, an AI Learning coach, and a marketing campaign tool. In the legal sphere, Michael Lewis's book "Going Infinite" details Sam Bankman-Fried's attempt to buy off Trump, debuting on the first day of his trial. The SBF trial in the Southern District of New York continues, with the prosecution accusing Bankman-Fried of fraud while the defense claims good faith. Chain Reaction and TechCrunch+ have covered the trial, while TechCrunch+ also highlighted Breathe Battery Technologies, a startup developing software to improve lithium-ion battery charging speeds and longevity. On social media, Elon Musk stated X generated 100–200 million posts daily in September, conflicting with CEO Linda Yaccarino's claim of 500 million posts per day. In the startup world, Dave Clark criticized Flexport's founder and board after being ousted as CEO. IronNet, founded in 2014 by former NSA director Keith Alexander, collapsed after failing to gain traction despite raising over $400 million. Esther Rodriguez-Villegas, founder of Acurable, discusses her company's wearable devices for diagnosing and managing respiratory conditions like sleep apnea and epilepsy. Gmail is also set to announce stricter rules for bulk senders starting next year.
