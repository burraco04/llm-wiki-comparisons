Eddy Cue testified in US v. Google, the antitrust trial over Google's search business, defending the Information Services Agreement (ISA) which makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices. The ISA has been in place since 2002, with Cue negotiating the current iteration with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016. During renegotiation, Apple sought a higher revenue percentage from users directed to the search engine, while Pichai wanted to keep the deal as is; they compromised on an undisclosed number.

Cue stated there was no valid alternative to Google at the time and there still isn't one, arguing Apple never seriously considered switching providers or building its own search product. Apple also argues that switching search engines is easy, contradicting DuckDuckGo's claims, and downplays Google's financial role in the deal, citing platform appeal instead. Additionally, Apple claims better privacy than Google and notes that Google integration was a key Safari innovation since before 2003.
