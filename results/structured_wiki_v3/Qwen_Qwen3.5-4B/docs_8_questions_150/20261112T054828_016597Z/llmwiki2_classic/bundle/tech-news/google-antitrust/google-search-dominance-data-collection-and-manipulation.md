Google's market dominance is attributed to its scale, longevity, ubiquity, and unmatched user data collection, which includes searches, smart speaker interactions, clicks on results and ads, enabling personalized services. While switching search providers is temporarily inconvenient due to loss of familiarity, users frequently revert to Google, making it the top Bing query in 2021 despite Microsoft defaults. Kent Walker defended Google's preference as a result of being the best rather than default status, citing intense industry competition.

Controversies surround allegations that Google manipulates search queries for ad revenue. Megan Grey, former DuckDuckGo vice-president, claimed Google replaces neutral terms like 'children's clothing' with monetizable brands like 'NIKOLAI-brand kidswear,' though she admitted possibly misinterpreting evidence. Google denies pushing users toward monetizable subjects, stating that ad keyword matching is a separate, long-standing process from the meaning-matching systems used for organic results. Wired removed an article on this topic for not meeting standards, but its widespread sharing suggests public skepticism regarding privacy and data handling.

Search quality has improved significantly due to data volume growth from approximately 9 ZB in 2013 to 120 ZB in 2023, shifting from static websites to live information within apps. The US faces an uphill legal battle proving Google broke the law to achieve its current market status quo. Additionally, Bing has attempted to compete with generative AI chatbot features and layout overhauls, though functionality remains limited compared to Google's established ecosystem.
