Michael Lewis met Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF) in early 2021 in Berkeley to evaluate his character before a financial commitment, meeting him more than 100 times over the next two years. SBF raised approximately $2 billion from backers including Sequoia, Temasek, and Tiger Global, including $1 billion in 2021, making him the richest person under 30 with $22.5 billion in assets. To lend legitimacy, SBF secured lucrative marketing deals with Tom Brady ($55 million) and Steph Curry ($35 million), and attempted to sponsor Anna Wintour's Met Costume Ball via Zoom. SBF also met with Mitch McConnell to discuss funding Republican candidates to offset Trump's influence, concocting a plan to offer $5 billion to Trump to cancel his presidential re-run, though no wire transfer was confirmed. FTX trading volume climbed to $15 billion per day, but valuations fell sharply after being based on speculation rather than banked value. SBF is currently denied bail, blamed for Alameda/FTX losses, and accused of causing a ripple effect in crypto woes. Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite' is set to debut on the first day of SBF's trial, which is scheduled for Tuesday at the Manhattan Federal Court, where SBF pleaded not-guilty to seven charges of fraud and conspiracy.
