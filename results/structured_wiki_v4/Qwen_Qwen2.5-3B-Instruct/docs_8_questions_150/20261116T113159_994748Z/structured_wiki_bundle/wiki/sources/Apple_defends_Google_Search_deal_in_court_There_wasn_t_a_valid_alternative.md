# Apple defends Google Search deal in court: ‘There wasn’t a valid alternative’

## Core Content

In the US v. Google antitrust trial, Eddy Cue, Apple's Senior Vice President of Internet Software & Services, testified about the Information Services Agreement (ISA) with Google, which makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices. The deal has been in place since 2002, and Apple negotiated a higher revenue share with Google in 2016. During his testimony, Cue emphasized that Apple believed in Google's product quality and prioritized user privacy, stating there was no viable alternative to Google at the time.

## Key Points

- Eddy Cue testified about the Information Services Agreement (ISA) with Google
- The ISA makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices
- Apple negotiated a higher revenue share with Google in 2016
- Cue emphasized Apple's belief in Google's product quality and user privacy
- Cue stated there was no viable alternative to Google at the time

## Facts and Data

- The Information Services Agreement (ISA) has been in place since 2002
- Apple negotiated a higher revenue share with Google in 2016
- Google pays Apple billions of dollars annually due to the ISA
- The deal ensures Google remains the default search engine on Apple devices
- Apple believes in protecting users' privacy and prioritizes it over economic interests
