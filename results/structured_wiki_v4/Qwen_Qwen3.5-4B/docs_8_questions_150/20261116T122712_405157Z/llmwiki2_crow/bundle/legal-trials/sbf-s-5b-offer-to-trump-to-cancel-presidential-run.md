Sam Bankman-Fried (SBF), the disgraced cryptocurrency entrepreneur accused of orchestrating the multibillion-dollar collapse of FTX, reportedly offered Donald Trump $5 billion to cancel his presidential re-run. No wire transfer was confirmed for this offer. This political fundraising activity occurred alongside SBF's legal troubles; he pleaded not guilty to seven charges of fraud and conspiracy in the Manhattan Federal Court trial. SBF had previously met with Mitch McConnell to fund Republican candidates offsetting Trump's influence. His rise involved raising approximately $2 billion, including $1 billion in 2021, from backers like Sequoia, Temasek, and Tiger Global. Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite' chronicles SBF's rise and fall, noting SBF was the richest person under 30 with $22.5 billion at the time of their meetings. FTX's marketing included deals with athletes like Tom Brady ($55 million) and Steph Curry ($35 million), and trading volume climbed to $15 billion daily. Following the crash, FTX valuations fell sharply due to reliance on speculation rather than banked value, and SBF has been denied bail while widely blamed for causing a ripple effect of crypto woes.
