Eddy Cue testified in the US v. Google antitrust trial defending the Information Services Agreement (ISA), which makes Google the default search engine on Apple devices. Although the ISA has existed since 2002, Cue negotiated the current iteration with Google CEO Sundar Pichai in 2016. During renegotiation, Apple sought a higher revenue share, while Pichai wanted to maintain the deal; they compromised on an undisclosed percentage.

Cue stated there was no valid alternative to Google at the time and that this remains true today. He argued the deal was driven by factors beyond economics, noting Apple never seriously considered switching providers or building its own search product. Regarding user experience, Cue acknowledged the ISA did not allow Apple to offer search engine choice during setup, reasoning that showing engines users had not heard of would be detrimental. He further stated Apple makes Google the default because it is the best, while acknowledging users can easily change it, contrasting this with DuckDuckGo's CEO testimony that switching takes too many steps.

On privacy, Cue agreed Apple prioritizes user privacy and offers better protection than Google, citing ISA provisions requiring login-free searching and Apple's efforts to prevent tracking. When questioned about the ISA's impact on Apple's profits, Bellshaw suggested it might be significant, but Cue replied that Apple does not view it that way because the agreement succeeds due to the platform's overall appeal. Additionally, Google lawyer John Schmidtlein led Cue through Safari's history, highlighting seamless Google integration as a key user interface innovation mentioned in early promotional materials before 2003. Cue explained that pre-2003 users had to type specific URLs to search, whereas Apple introduced functionality allowing non-URL text in the URL field to trigger searches. Schmidtlein also argued Google helped Safari succeed by being a great product, referencing deals with Yahoo and Bing, and both men claimed switching search engines is easy and a non-issue.
