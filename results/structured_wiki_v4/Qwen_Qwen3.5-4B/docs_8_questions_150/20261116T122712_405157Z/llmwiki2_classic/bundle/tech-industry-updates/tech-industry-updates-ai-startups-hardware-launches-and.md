Induced AI, founded this year by two teenagers, assists businesses in automating workflows by converting plain English back-office task instructions to pseudo-code in real time. Google unveiled the Pixel 8, Pixel 8 Pro, Pixel Fold, Android 14, Pixel Buds, Google Assistant, Bard, and Pixel Watch 2 at its annual hardware event. Flexport, a freight forwarding and customs brokerage startup valued at $8 billion, saw former Amazon executive Dave Clark ousted as CEO just a year into the job; he later criticized the company's founder and board following a CNBC report. In legal news, Sam Bankman-Fried is accused of attempting to buy off Donald Trump to prevent him from running for president again, according to Michael Lewis's book 'Going Infinite.' Acurable, a medical device company founded by Esther Rodriguez-Villegas, creates wearable devices to diagnose and manage respiratory conditions like sleep apnea and epilepsy.
