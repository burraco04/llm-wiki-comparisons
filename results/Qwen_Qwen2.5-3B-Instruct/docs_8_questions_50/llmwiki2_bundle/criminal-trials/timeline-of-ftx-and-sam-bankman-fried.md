# Overview

## Rise and Fall

FTX, founded by Sam Bankman-Fried, experienced rapid growth and then faced severe financial troubles. Key events include the company's initial launch, its peak in popularity, and the subsequent collapse. Sam Bankman-Fried was arrested in December 2022 in the Bahamas and later extradited to the United States. He was released on a $250 million bail bond and faced multiple charges, including wire fraud and conspiracy to commit money laundering. The case involves significant legal proceedings and affects numerous investors and creditors.

## Creation of 'Going Infinite'

In an interview broadcast by 60 Minutes, Michael Lewis, the author of 'Going Infinite,' revealed that he was introduced to Sam Bankman-Fried in early 2021 by a friend who wanted to back FTX. Despite no details on the investment, Lewis was so impressed with Bankman-Fried's brilliance that he decided to write a book about him. Over the next eighteen months, they met more than 100 times, and Lewis documented their interactions, which eventually led to the creation of 'Going Infinite.'

### Behind-the-Scenes

During the filming of 'Going Infinite,' Lewis shared that he observed Bankman-Fried during his first television appearance, noting that his eyes were constantly moving back and forth, indicating his focus on the game he was playing while on camera.
