Google's search functionality relies heavily on keyword matching to provide relevant results. However, the increasing volume of user data collected through various interactions (searches, smart speakers, etc.) influences the ranking of search results. Machine learning algorithms are used to analyze this data, potentially leading to biased or personalized search outcomes. This raises concerns about the fairness of Google's search results, especially when considering the impact on competitors and users' privacy rights.
