The lawsuit, filed in 2023, accuses Google and Alphabet of anticompetitive behavior, specifically targeting their AI technologies. Key points include:

- **Search Generative Experience (SGE)** and **Bard AI chatbot** are highlighted as contributing to the issue.
- The lawsuit argues that these technologies shift traffic away from publishers' websites, potentially leading to significant losses in ad revenue and website traffic.
- The plaintiffs request an injunction requiring Google to obtain consent from publishers to use their website data to train AI products and allow publishers who opt out of SGE to still appear in Google search results.
- The lawsuit invokes the Sherman Act and Clayton Act to seek class-wide monetary and injunctive relief to restore competition for digital news and reference publishing.
