# Apple's Decision to Use Google Search

- Apple chose Google Search as the default due to its perceived quality and ease of use.
- Apple argues that showing users unfamiliar search engines would be a poor user experience.
- Apple emphasizes the importance of user privacy and cites Google's privacy policies as a reason for preferring Google.
- Apple maintains that the integration between Safari and Google Search is seamless and beneficial for users.
- The Justice Department questions whether Google is the best search engine and what the market would look like without Google's financial support.
